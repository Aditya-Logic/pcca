      How access works

      Central administrator

      Principal Accounts Office (PRAO), MHA — manages the portal, office records, report consolidation, moderation and access.

      Office user

      PAO / RPAO / ZPAO / other office — submits its own reports and maintains its own office information. It does not manage other offices.

      Office access

      “Enabled” means an office can sign in. “Not configured” means no office-level account has been enabled yet.
